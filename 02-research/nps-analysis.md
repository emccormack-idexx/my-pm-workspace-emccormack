# NPS Feedback Findings — Streak & Re-engagement

*For: Marcus. Source: 10 raw NPS free-text comments. Same underlying signal as the 3 user interviews (see `interview-synthesis.md`) — this shows it holds at slightly broader scale.*

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once (ranked by frequency)

| Rank | Theme | Mentions | Comments |
|------|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Streak loss triggers disengagement or churn | 4 | "reset to zero...haven't opened since"; "start from scratch"; "deleted after 3 weeks"; "the second I lost it, I was done" |
| 2 | No recovery mechanism — users want a streak-freeze | 3 | "no way to recover it. Other apps let you freeze a streak"; "coach...not a scorekeeper"; "make coming back easier" |
| 3 | Notification/re-engagement calibration is off | 3 | "daily reminder...feels like nagging"; "notifications feel random...turned them all off"; "forget it exists after a couple of days" |
| 4 | No acknowledgment of the user's current state | 3 | "home screen looks the same whether I'm on a 2-day streak or coming back after two weeks"; "coach...not a scorekeeper"; "make coming back easier...instead of making me feel like I failed" |
| 5 | Lesson content quality is a strength | 2 | "the first week was genuinely fun"; "love the lessons" |

*Note: several comments touch more than one theme (e.g., the "coach, not scorekeeper" comment speaks to both the recovery-mechanism theme and the personalization theme) — counted under each relevant theme rather than forced into one bucket.*

## Praise vs. Complaints

**Praise**
- Lesson content itself is well-liked and described as fun.
- One respondent's first week of onboarding was called out positively.

**Complaints**
- Losing a streak feels like punishment and is a direct churn trigger for multiple respondents.
- No mechanism exists to recover or protect a streak after a break (explicit comparison to competitor streak-freeze features).
- Notification strategy is inconsistent — some users feel nagged by frequency/timing, others forget the app exists entirely, suggesting outreach isn't well-targeted to where a user actually is in their journey.
- The app gives no visual or contextual acknowledgment of a user's current state (new streak vs. long streak vs. returning after time away).

## Top 3 Actionable Issues

1. **No recovery path after a streak break.** Cited directly as the reason for quitting by multiple respondents, and as the single biggest lever tying back to the Day-7 retention drop.
2. **No acknowledgment of user state on return.** The home screen treats a 2-day streak and a returning-after-two-weeks user identically — a low-context, high-impact fix.
3. **Miscalibrated notifications.** Currently a blunt instrument — too frequent for some, invisible for others — suggesting a need for state-aware timing rather than a flat cadence.

## Bottom Line for Marcus

This feedback independently confirms what we're seeing in the interview data: the streak-reset moment, not the daily lesson content, is where users are lost. The content itself isn't the problem — the comeback moment is. This directly supports the problem framing already in `project.md` / `strategy.md` and gives us broader (though still qualitative) evidence to bring into the squad alignment conversation.
