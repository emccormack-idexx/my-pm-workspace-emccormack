# Streakly Comeback Experience — Problem & Scope Alignment

*Pre-read for working team meeting. Goal: align on the problem and scope before this becomes a proposal to leadership. This doc intentionally does not propose a solution — that's for the next phase.*

## Problem Statement

Day-7 retention has dropped from 48% to 39% since the streak redesign shipped. The decline is sharpest among users who break their streak in week 1 — once a user misses two consecutive days, churn nearly doubles.

User research points to why: people build a streak, miss a day for normal reasons, and return to a counter reset to zero with no acknowledgment of their prior progress. The "you lost your streak" notification has a harsh tone, and following it leads users back to day zero with nothing else offered.

Working hypothesis: users disengage after a streak break because it feels like failure, and the app currently offers no path back in — just a reset.

Open question for this group: is the underlying cause the streak-reset mechanic itself, the notification's timing/tone, or both?

## Goals

- Recover Day-7 retention toward pre-redesign levels.
- Move away from generic re-engagement messaging toward something specific to each user's own progress.

## Non-Goals

- Not yet defined — this is one of the things we want to align on as a group.

## Success Metrics

- Day-7 retention (primary metric; no target set yet).
- Churn rate among users who miss two consecutive days (currently roughly 2x baseline) as a leading indicator.
